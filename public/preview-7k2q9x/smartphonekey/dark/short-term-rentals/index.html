How keys are issued

You send each key. Nothing syncs by itself.

This is the manual flow SmartphoneKey’s guides describe, from booking to check-out.

  1. A booking is confirmed

    Note the check-in and check-out times from your booking platform.

  2. Create a guest key

    In SmartphoneKey’s admin, set when the key starts and ends. Optionally limit how many times it works, or restrict it to the guest’s email.

  3. Send it

    Share the key by email or a messaging app. SmartphoneKey describes guest keys working as a link, or from Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

  4. It ends on time

    The key stops at the end time. Remove it early if plans change, and check the activity log to see when the door was opened.

Automatic reservation sync isn’t confirmed. If you need keys created from bookings automatically, compare smart locks that your booking platform connects to directly.

What changes

No key handover, no lockbox.

Guests get in without meeting you, cleaners get their own window, and you can see what happened.

Guests

In without a meeting.

  • The key arrives before the stay and works from check-in to check-out.
  • No physical key to lose or return.
  • A QR code at the door lets their visitors call.

Cleaners

Their own window.

  • A guest key for the cleaning window only.
  • One-use keys for one-off repair visits.
  • Remove any key the moment plans change.

You

Every door, one admin.

  • Manage access remotely in SmartphoneKey’s admin.
  • One guest key can cover several locks, as the admin guide describes.
  • An activity log shows when keys were accepted and doors unlocked.

Your rental door

What we check at a rental door.

SmartphoneKey switches electric locks, so the door, its power and the building around it come first.

  1. 1

    The lock and the frame

    Whether an electric strike or magnetic lock suits your door, and what the building allows.

  2. 2

    Power at the door

    The reader and the lock need low-voltage power, which many unit doors don’t have nearby.

  3. 3

    The cable route

    How wiring reaches the lock and power supply without damaging finished walls.

  4. 4

    A backup way in

    What guests and you do if a phone dies, a link is lost or the network drops.

  5. 5

    Wi-Fi or LTE

    Guest Wi-Fi can be switched off or unstable; the LTE model uses a SIM card instead.

  6. 6

    The building around the door

    Which door guests use to get in, and whether the building’s entrance is separate.

Condo unit? Your guests may still need to get through the lobby, and that door is the building’s decision. Building rules and short-term rental rules also apply separately from the lock.

Is it the right fit?

Good for some hosts. Not for every rental.

A good fit if

  • You manage several doors or units and want one admin for all of them.
  • You want guest keys, Wallet keys and cards side by side.
  • You want an activity log and QR visitor calls at the door.
  • You’re happy for a reader to be installed on an electric lock.

Maybe not if

  • You need keys created automatically from your bookings.
  • You only want a battery smart lock on one door that you fit yourself.
  • You can’t add power or change the door hardware.

Answers for hosts

What hosts ask first.

Starting with the one that matters most for a booking calendar.

Does it sync with Airbnb?

An automatic link with Airbnb or other booking platforms isn’t confirmed. Plan on creating and sending each guest key yourself from SmartphoneKey’s admin.

Do guests need an app?

SmartphoneKey describes guest keys working as a link on the guest’s phone, or from Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, without a separate app.

A guest checks in late or leaves early.

Remove the old key and send a new one with the new times.

Can cleaners use the guest’s key?

Give them their own guest key for the cleaning window instead, so each person’s access starts and ends on its own and shows separately in the activity log.

What if the Wi-Fi goes down?

SmartphoneKey’s admin guide says keys sent while a lock is offline wait until it reconnects. Consider the LTE model, and agree a backup way in during the door check.

Can I see when the guest arrived?

The activity log shows when keys were accepted and the door unlocked, and can be filtered by date and user.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of doors and keys and on any electrical work, so there is no price on this site. Cost is explained after the door check, including any ongoing costs.
