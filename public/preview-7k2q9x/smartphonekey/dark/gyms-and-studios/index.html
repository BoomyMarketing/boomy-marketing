What changes

Three people, one front door.

Members get an easier way in. Your team steps out from behind the door. You get control, and a record.

Members

Nothing extra to carry.

  • The key lives in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
  • Hold the phone to the reader and the door releases.
  • Prefer a card? Key cards work on the same reader.

Staff

Off door duty.

  • Members let themselves in, so the front desk isn’t the door.
  • Visitors without a key scan the QR code to call whoever is on shift.
  • Trial passes get a guest key that stops at the end time you set.

Owner

Control, with a record.

  • Add or remove anyone’s access in SmartphoneKey’s admin, or let local admins do it.
  • Guest keys with a start and end time and an optional limit on uses.
  • An activity log of keys accepted and doors unlocked.
  • Optional live view from compatible (ONVIF) cameras, as SmartphoneKey describes.

Your gym door

What we check at a gym entrance.

SmartphoneKey switches electric locks. Some gym entrances already have one; others need one added before phone access can work.

  1. 1

    The lock on your entrance

    Regular key, electric strike, magnetic lock, or an existing fob system you want to replace?

  2. 2

    Power at the door

    The reader and the lock need low-voltage power nearby.

  3. 3

    The cable route

    How wiring gets through the frame or wall to the lock and the power supply.

  4. 4

    How members leave

    An exit button is included. Exit and safety needs are confirmed on site.

  5. 5

    Wi-Fi or LTE

    Dependable Wi-Fi at the entrance, or the LTE model with a SIM card.

  6. 6

    Who needs keys

    Members, coaches, cleaners and trial visitors, and who would rather use a card.

Staffed and unstaffed hours. A reader decides who can open the door. It doesn’t stop someone following a member in, and it doesn’t replace a plan for unstaffed hours or emergencies. Talk these through at the door check.

SmartphoneKey’s admin guide describes a weekly schedule of access times for each lock. The door check confirms how that fits your opening hours.

Answers for gyms

What gym owners ask first.

Straight answers, including the ones that aren’t a simple yes.

Does it connect to our membership or billing software?

Not as a confirmed, ready-made link. Access is added and removed in SmartphoneKey’s admin. If you need it tied to your membership system, raise it at the door check so it can be looked into before you rely on it.

Will access switch off when a membership ends?

Not automatically from your billing system, as far as has been confirmed. You or your staff remove the key in the admin, and it stops working.

How is the cost worked out for a lot of members?

It depends on the number of doors and keys and on any electrical work. Ask for the full cost for your actual number of members before deciding; there is no price on this site.

What about members who don’t want to use a phone?

SmartphoneKey key cards work on the same reader. They are programmed and revoked in the admin.

Can staff manage keys without the owner?

SmartphoneKey’s admin guide describes local admins who manage users and keys for a site, so the owner doesn’t have to do every change.

We have more than one location.

The admin guide describes separate entities for each site, each with its own doors, users and admins. The door check covers every entrance you want connected.

How do drop-ins and trial classes get in?

Send a guest key with a start and end time, and limit it to one use if you like. Visitors without a key can scan the QR code at the door to call your desk.
