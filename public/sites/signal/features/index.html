Keys

Every kind of key a door needs.

  • Apple Wallet & Google Wallet keys

    The key lives in the phone’s Wallet. People tap it on the reader, with no separate app for everyday entry.

  • Guest keys with start and end times

    Give a visitor a key that starts and ends when you set. Limit how many times it opens, or tie it to one email.

  • One-use keys

    A key for a single visit, such as a repair or a delivery. It opens once, then it is spent.

  • NFC key cards

    Physical cards for people who would rather not use a phone. Cards are programmed and revoked from the admin portal.

  • One key, several doors

    A single key can cover more than one lock, for example a lobby and a unit door, or a front door and a back door.

  • Weekly lock schedules

    Set the hours each lock accepts keys, day by day, and sync the schedule to the door.

  • Keys reach offline locks

    If a lock is offline when you issue a key, the key waits as pending and is delivered when the lock is back online.

  • Phone access by app

    People can also be given access through the SmartphoneKey mobile app, alongside or instead of a Wallet key.

Visitors

People without a key can still get in, with your say-so.

  • QR intercom at the door

    Visitors scan the QR code on the reader with their phone camera. It opens in the browser and connects them to the right person to ask for entry.

  • Extra QR stickers

    Two QR stickers come in the box, for wherever visitors will look first.

People & sites

Run one door or many buildings from one place.

  • Remove access in seconds

    Remove a key, or deactivate a person entirely: their lock access ends and their portal sign-in is disabled.

  • One-tap actions

    The dashboard has quick buttons to give access, send a guest key or remove access, plus live counts of locks, keys and people.

  • Sites and buildings

    Group doors and people by building, site or location, and switch between them from one admin portal.

  • Local admins

    Let a manager, front desk or superintendent run one site. You can hand them a set number of Wallet Keys to give out.

  • People directory

    Find anyone by name, email, phone, unit number or licence plate. Search by a unit number and that unit comes first.

  • Bulk import

    On larger plans, add many people at once from a CSV or Excel file.

Records & control

Know who came in, and who changed what.

  • Activity log

    Every key accepted, door unlocked, and key added or removed, filterable by lock, person, event and date.

  • Audit log

    A separate record of what admins did: who issued or removed which key, for whom, and when.

  • Notifications

    Choose which events you are notified about in the admin portal settings.

  • Installer access you approve

    An installer requests temporary access to specific locks. Nothing happens until the owner approves it, for the time they choose.

Doors & devices

The hardware side: locks, network, cameras and smart home.

  • Live lock status

    Each lock shows whether it is online or offline, so problems are visible before someone is stuck outside.

  • Wi-Fi or built-in LTE

    The Wi-Fi model joins your network. The LTE model uses a SIM card where Wi-Fi does not reach, or on temporary sites.

  • Cameras and camera roles

    Connect ONVIF-compatible cameras for live footage, and decide which people can see which cameras.

  • Matter hubs and devices

    SmartphoneKey supports Matter. Hubs and Matter devices, such as sensors, can be managed in the same portal.

  • Your electric lock

    The reader’s built-in relay switches an electric strike or a magnetic lock. Which suits your door is decided at the door check.

The admin portal

Where it all happens.

  • Admin portal on any device

    Manage everything from a browser on a computer or phone. Sign in with email, Google or Apple.

  • Six languages

    The portal works in English, French, Spanish, German, Ukrainian and Greek.

Answers

About the features.

Is every feature included?

Some sections of the admin portal, such as cameras, Matter devices, guest keys and intercom access, depend on the subscription package. The door check covers which package fits.

Do people need an app?

No separate app for everyday entry. Keys live in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet; app access is also available.

Does it sync with booking, membership or HR software?

An automatic link isn’t confirmed. Keys are issued and removed in SmartphoneKey’s admin portal.

Which of these does your door need?

The door check covers the door, the lock and the features that fit how you use it.
