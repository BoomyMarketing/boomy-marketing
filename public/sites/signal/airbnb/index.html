In short

How do Airbnb guests check in with a phone key?

For Airbnb hosts and co-hosts

For each reservation the host sends the guest a key that starts at check-in and ends at check-out. The guest opens the door with Apple Wallet, Google Wallet or a link, the cleaner gets a separate key for the turnover window, and the activity log shows when people arrived. Keys are sent from SmartphoneKey's admin portal: an automatic link with Airbnb's calendar isn't confirmed.

What you get

The features that matter for airbnb hosts.

  • Guest keys with start and end times

    Give a visitor a key that starts and ends when you set. Limit how many times it opens, or tie it to one email.

  • One-use keys

    A key for a single visit, such as a repair or a delivery. It opens once, then it is spent.

  • Apple Wallet & Google Wallet keys

    The key lives in the phone’s Wallet. People tap it on the reader, with no separate app for everyday entry.

  • Remove access in seconds

    Remove a key, or deactivate a person entirely: their lock access ends and their portal sign-in is disabled.

  • Local admins

    Let a manager, front desk or superintendent run one site. You can hand them a set number of Wallet Keys to give out.

  • One key, several doors

    A single key can cover more than one lock, for example a lobby and a unit door, or a front door and a back door.

  • Activity log

    Every key accepted, door unlocked, and key added or removed, filterable by lock, person, event and date.

  • Keys reach offline locks

    If a lock is offline when you issue a key, the key waits as pending and is delivered when the lock is back online.

  • QR intercom at the door

    Visitors scan the QR code on the reader with their phone camera. It opens in the browser and connects them to the right person to ask for entry.

Answers

What airbnb hosts ask first.

Does it connect to my Airbnb calendar?

An automatic link with Airbnb isn’t confirmed. You or a co-host send each guest key from the admin portal, with the stay’s dates and times.

What if a guest arrives late or wants to stay longer?

The key works for the whole stay, whenever they arrive. To extend a stay, change the key’s end time from your phone.

Can my cleaner get in between stays?

Yes. Give the cleaner their own guest key that works only during the turnover window.

Do guests need to install an app?

No. A guest key works from a link, or from Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

Is short-term renting allowed at my address?

That depends on the City of Toronto’s short-term rental rules and your building’s rules. A door reader doesn’t change them, so check before you list.
