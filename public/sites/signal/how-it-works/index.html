Start to finish

Five steps, in this order.

  1. 1

    Door check

    The local team looks at the lock, power, cable route, Wi-Fi or LTE and who needs keys, then explains setup and cost.

  2. 2

    Installation

    The reader is mounted beside the door and wired to an electric strike or magnetic lock, with an exit button on the inside.

  3. 3

    Claim the lock

    The lock is registered to your account with its serial number and Matter pairing code, then appears in your admin portal.

  4. 4

    Add people

    Add people one by one or, on larger plans, from a spreadsheet. Send Wallet keys, guest keys or program key cards.

  5. 5

    Tap and manage

    People tap their phone to open the door. You watch the activity log and change access whenever you need to.

The reader

Slim on the frame. Serious behind it.

  • Power12–24 V DC low-voltage power, ideally from the same supply as the lock.
  • Built-in relaySwitches an electric strike or magnetic lock.
  • Exit buttonA push-to-exit button for the inside comes in the box.
  • Wi-Fi modelJoins your network during setup.
  • LTE modelUses a SIM card where Wi-Fi isn’t practical.
  • In the boxReader with backplate, exit button with backplate, wall-mount kit, hidden-bolt screwdriver and two QR stickers.

The door check

Every setup starts with your door.

Installers and owners

You stay in charge of your locks.

  • Installer access you approve

    An installer requests temporary access to specific locks. Nothing happens until the owner approves it, for the time they choose.

  • Live lock status

    Each lock shows whether it is online or offline, so problems are visible before someone is stuck outside.

  • Keys reach offline locks

    If a lock is offline when you issue a key, the key waits as pending and is delivered when the lock is back online.

  • Audit log

    A separate record of what admins did: who issued or removed which key, for whom, and when.

Answers

About installation.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the door, the lock and the wiring. The door check gives you the scope before anything is booked.

Can I keep my existing electric lock?

If it is an electric strike or magnetic lock that suits the reader, it may be reused. That is confirmed on site.

What if Wi-Fi doesn’t reach the door?

The LTE model uses a SIM card instead.

Who can change my locks after installation?

You do. An installer needs your approval for temporary access to specific locks.
