What is phone door access for a house?

For Homeowners and families

Each family member opens the front door with a key in their phone’s Wallet. Sitters, dog walkers and trades get guest keys with start and end dates, and anyone without a phone can use a key card. A house door usually needs an electric lock and power added first, which the door check confirms.

    What you get

    The features that matter for homes.

    • Apple Wallet & Google Wallet keys

      The key lives in the phone’s Wallet. People tap it on the reader, with no separate app for everyday entry.

    • Guest keys with start and end times

      Give a visitor a key that starts and ends when you set. Limit how many times it opens, or tie it to one email.

    • One-use keys

      A key for a single visit, such as a repair or a delivery. It opens once, then it is spent.

    • NFC key cards

      Physical cards for people who would rather not use a phone. Cards are programmed and revoked from the admin portal.

    • Remove access in seconds

      Remove a key, or deactivate a person entirely: their lock access ends and their portal sign-in is disabled.

    • Activity log

      Every key accepted, door unlocked, and key added or removed, filterable by lock, person, event and date.

    • Matter hubs and devices

      SmartphoneKey supports Matter. Hubs and Matter devices, such as sensors, can be managed in the same portal.

    • Cameras and camera roles

      Connect ONVIF-compatible cameras for live footage, and decide which people can see which cameras.

    • Wi-Fi or built-in LTE

      The Wi-Fi model joins your network. The LTE model uses a SIM card where Wi-Fi does not reach, or on temporary sites.

    Answers

    What homeowners ask first.

    Does it work with Google Home or Apple Home?

    SmartphoneKey supports Matter, so it can sit alongside Matter-based smart home systems. The exact setup is confirmed at the door check.

    What about family members without a phone?

    An NFC key card works on the same reader.

    Will it work with my front door?

    The reader switches an electric strike or magnetic lock. A door with only a regular key will likely need an electric lock, power and wiring first.

    Can I see who came in?

    Yes. The activity log shows every key accepted and every door unlocked.
