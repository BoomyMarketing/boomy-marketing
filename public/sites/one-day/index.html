    How it works

    Three steps from a metal key to a phone.

    1. 1

      The reader goes on your door

      After a door check, the SmartphoneKey reader is mounted at the entrance and wired to the door’s electric lock.

    2. 2

      The key lands in the Wallet

      You send each person a key. It sits in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, with no separate app to install.

    3. 3

      A tap opens the door

      They hold the phone to the reader, the way they tap to pay. You can change or remove the key at any time.

    Answers

    What people ask first.

    Do people need an app?

    No separate app for everyday entry. Keys live in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, and guest keys can be opened from a link. Visitors use their phone camera to scan the QR code.

    Will it work with the lock I have?

    The reader switches an electric strike or a magnetic lock. A door that only takes a regular key will likely need an electric lock, power and wiring first. That is checked in person.

    What if Wi-Fi doesn’t reach the door?

    The LTE model uses a SIM card and mobile signal instead of Wi-Fi.

    Can I see who came in?

    Yes. The activity log shows keys accepted, doors unlocked, and keys added or removed.

    Does it sync with Airbnb or gym software?

    An automatic link with booking platforms or membership software isn’t confirmed. You issue and remove keys in SmartphoneKey’s admin.

    What does it cost?

    It depends on the door, the lock, power, wiring and how many doors you connect. The local team explains setup and cost after the door check.

    Is this SmartphoneKey’s own website?

    No. This is a local team that assesses and installs SmartphoneKey in Toronto and the GTA.
