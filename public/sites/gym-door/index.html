Door: unlocking

A tap, and the lock lets go.

The reader on the frame checks the key and switches the electric lock. Three ways to give it a key:

  • Wallet key Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. Tap like paying.
  • NFC key card For anyone who would rather not use a phone.
  • QR code Visitors scan it to call the person they’re here to see.

Door: open for members

Members let themselves in.

Each member gets a Wallet key that works until you remove it. Nobody at the desk has to buzz the door at 6 a.m.

Door: open for staff

Coaches open up. On their hours.

The coach’s key works on the side door, and it stops working the day she leaves. A weekly schedule decides the hours each door accepts keys.

Door: open · you’re inside

Step inside. We start at your door.

Before anything is installed, we come out and look at the door, the lock and the power. Then you know what it takes.

  1. The lock you haveRegular key only, electric strike, magnetic lock or an old fob system.
  2. Power at the doorThe reader and the lock need low-voltage power nearby.
  3. The cable routeHow wiring reaches the lock and the power supply.
  4. Wi-Fi or LTEDependable Wi-Fi at the door, or the LTE model with a SIM card.
  5. Who needs keysMembers, coaches, cleaners and trial visitors, and who would rather use a card.

A reader decides who can open the door. It does not stop someone following a member in, and it does not replace a plan for unstaffed hours. Talk these through at the door check.

What gym owners ask first.

Does it connect to our membership or billing software?

A ready-made link isn’t confirmed. Plan on managing access in SmartphoneKey’s own admin portal, and bring it up at the door check.

Will access switch off when a membership ends?

When you remove the key or deactivate the person, their access ends. It doesn’t happen automatically from your billing system.

What about members who don’t want to use a phone?

Program an NFC key card for them. Cards work on the same reader and are revoked from the admin portal.

Can staff manage keys without the owner?

Yes. Make a manager a local admin for your location, and give them a set number of Wallet Keys to hand out.

We have more than one location.

Each location can be its own site in the admin portal, and you switch between them from one sign-in.
