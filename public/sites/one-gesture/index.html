A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before the
first tap.

Will it work with my door?+

SmartphoneKey switches a compatible electric lock. The existing door, lock, power and connectivity need to be checked before confirming a setup.

Can guests have temporary access?+

Give guests access with a start and end time. Manage their keys alongside the people who use your door every day.

Do I need a separate phone app?+

Keep your door key in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. A physical NFC key card is another option.
