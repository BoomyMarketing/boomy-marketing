In short

What is phone door access for an office?

For office managers, coworking operators and business owners

Team members and coworking members open the door with a key in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. Each door can follow a weekly schedule, clients reach the right person through a QR code on the reader, and access is removed the day someone leaves. Every entry and every admin change is recorded.

What you get

The features that matter here.

Picked for offices & coworking from everything the reader and admin portal can do.

  • Keys

    Apple Wallet & Google Wallet keys

    The key lives in the phone’s Wallet. People tap it on the reader, with no separate app for everyday entry.

  • Keys

    Weekly lock schedules

    Set the hours each lock accepts keys, day by day, and sync the schedule to the door.

  • Visitors

    QR intercom at the door

    Visitors scan the QR code on the reader with their phone camera. It opens in the browser and connects them to the right person to ask for entry.

  • Keys

    One key, several doors

    A single key can cover more than one lock, for example a lobby and a unit door, or a front door and a back door.

  • Keys

    Guest keys with start and end times

    Give a visitor a key that starts and ends when you set. Limit how many times it opens, or tie it to one email.

  • People & sites

    Remove access in seconds

    Remove a key, or deactivate a person entirely: their lock access ends and their portal sign-in is disabled.

  • People & sites

    Local admins

    Let a manager, front desk or superintendent run one site. You can hand them a set number of Wallet Keys to give out.

  • People & sites

    Sites and buildings

    Group doors and people by building, site or location, and switch between them from one admin portal.

  • Records & control

    Activity log

    Every key accepted, door unlocked, and key added or removed, filterable by lock, person, event and date.

  • Records & control

    Audit log

    A separate record of what admins did: who issued or removed which key, for whom, and when.

The door check

What we check at your door.

SmartphoneKey switches electric locks. Whether your door already has one, or needs one, decides what the job involves.

  1. 1

    The lock you have

    Regular key only, electric strike, magnetic lock or an old fob system.

  2. 2

    Power at the door

    The reader and the lock need low-voltage power nearby.

  3. 3

    The cable route

    How wiring reaches the lock and the power supply.

  4. 4

    How people leave

    An exit button is included; exit and safety needs are confirmed on site.

  5. 5

    Wi-Fi or LTE

    Dependable Wi-Fi at the door, or the LTE model with a SIM card.

  6. 6

    Existing fobs

    If an old fob system is on the door, whether the reader replaces it is decided on site.

Leasing an office in a larger building? The building’s own entrance is the landlord’s decision. Your suite door can usually be checked separately.

Is it the right fit?

Good for many. Not for every door.

A good fit if

  • People join and leave often, or work odd hours.
  • Clients and couriers arrive at a door nobody watches.
  • You run several floors, suites or locations.

Maybe not if

  • Your landlord won’t allow changes to the suite door.
  • You need access synced automatically from HR or booking software. That isn’t confirmed.

Answers for offices

What offices & coworking ask first.

Straight answers, including the ones that aren’t a simple yes.

Can a new hire get a key on their first day?

Yes. Add them in the admin portal and send their key; it lands in their phone’s Wallet.

Can we limit access to working hours?

Each lock can have a weekly schedule of the hours it accepts keys.

How do clients get in?

They scan the QR code on the reader and connect to the right person to ask for entry.

We run a coworking space with several suites.

One key can cover several locks, and each location can be its own site in the admin portal with its own local admin.

Is a phone key secure?

Keys sit in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet on each person’s own phone, can be limited to set times and can be removed at any moment. The activity log shows every entry, and a separate audit log shows which admin issued or removed which key.

Who can see the entry records?

Entry records stay in SmartphoneKey’s admin portal: an activity log of keys accepted and doors unlocked, and an audit log of admin changes. You decide who is an admin and who can see which camera. The local installer’s privacy terms will be published before launch.

I rent. Can I still do this?

Talk to your landlord or property manager first: changing a door needs the owner’s approval, and a shared entrance is the building’s decision (in a condo, the board’s). Owners decide for their own doors; condo-unit owners should check the building’s rules.
