In short

How does a QR intercom work?

For buildings, offices and businesses with visitors

Instead of a keypad or a wired buzzer panel, the SmartphoneKey reader carries a QR code. A visitor scans it with their phone camera, it opens in the browser, and it connects them to the right person to ask for entry. The same reader accepts Wallet keys and NFC cards from people who belong, and two extra QR stickers can go wherever visitors look first.

At the door

Visitors call. Keys open.

One reader handles both the people with keys and the people without.

  • QR visitor calls

    Visitors scan the code with the phone camera and connect to the person they’re visiting. No app needed.

  • People directory

    Find anyone by name, email, phone or unit number; search a unit number and that unit comes first.

  • Wallet keys and cards

    People who belong tap a phone or an NFC card on the same reader.

  • Guest keys for expected visitors

    Send a key with a start and end time, or a one-use key for a delivery.

  • Cameras

    Connect ONVIF-compatible cameras and choose who can see which camera.

  • Extra QR stickers

    Two stickers in the box, for wherever visitors will look first.

Is it the right fit?

Good for many. Not for every door.

A good fit if

  • You want visitors handled at the door without a wired buzzer panel.
  • Residents or staff should open the door with their phones.
  • You want a record of entries at the door.

Maybe not if

  • You need a voice panel with buttons for every unit.
  • You need the intercom tied into another property system. That isn’t confirmed.

Answers

What people ask first.

Straight answers, including the ones that aren’t a simple yes.

Do visitors need an app to call?

No. SmartphoneKey says visitors scan the QR code with the phone camera, and it opens in the browser.

What happens after a visitor calls?

They connect to the right person to ask for entry. How entry is then given at your door is set up with you at the door check.

Can it replace our old intercom?

Whether the reader replaces an existing intercom or buzzer, and what can stay, is decided on site. Exact compatibility needs a local check.

Is intercom included in every plan?

Some sections of the admin portal, including intercom access, depend on the subscription package. The door check covers which package fits.

What happens in a power cut?

The reader and the electric lock run on low-voltage power. In an outage, a magnetic lock releases, and an electric strike stays locked or releases depending on the model chosen. How your door should behave, and how people get out, is decided at the door check; ask about a backup power supply there too.

Who can see the entry records?

Entry records stay in SmartphoneKey’s admin portal: an activity log of keys accepted and doors unlocked, and an audit log of admin changes. You decide who is an admin and who can see which camera. The local installer’s privacy terms will be published before launch.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the door. A door that already has an electric lock and power nearby is a smaller job than one that needs a lock, power and a new cable route. You get the timing with the cost after the door check, before any work starts.
