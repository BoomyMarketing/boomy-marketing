In short

What is phone door access for a house?

For homeowners and families

Each family member opens the front door with a key in their phone’s Wallet. Sitters, dog walkers and trades get guest keys with start and end dates, and anyone without a phone can use a key card. A house door usually needs an electric lock and power added first, which the door check confirms.

Picked for homes from everything the reader and admin portal can do.

    The key lives in the phone’s Wallet. People tap it on the reader, with no separate app for everyday entry.

The door check

What we check at your door.

SmartphoneKey switches electric locks. Whether your door already has one, or needs one, decides what the job involves.

  1. 1

    The lock you have

    Regular key only, electric strike, magnetic lock or an old fob system.

  2. 2

    Power at the door

    The reader and the lock need low-voltage power nearby.

  3. 3

    The cable route

    How wiring reaches the lock and the power supply.

  4. 4

    How people leave

    An exit button is included; exit and safety needs are confirmed on site.

  5. 5

    Wi-Fi or LTE

    Dependable Wi-Fi at the door, or the LTE model with a SIM card.

  6. 6

    House or condo unit

    A house door is yours. In a condo, the lobby belongs to the building.

Most front doors only take a regular key today. Phone access then needs an electric lock, power and wiring first. That work is scoped at the door check.

Is it the right fit?

Good for many. Not for every door.

A good fit if

  • Several people and helpers need your front door.
  • You want guest keys that end by themselves.
  • You already use Matter devices at home.

Maybe not if

  • You want a battery smart lock you fit yourself in ten minutes.
  • You can’t add power or wiring at the door.

Answers for homes

What homeowners ask first.

Straight answers, including the ones that aren’t a simple yes.

Does it work with Google Home or Apple Home?

SmartphoneKey supports Matter, so it can sit alongside Matter-based smart home systems. The exact setup is confirmed at the door check.

What about family members without a phone?

An NFC key card works on the same reader.

Will it work with my front door?

The reader switches an electric strike or magnetic lock. A door with only a regular key will likely need an electric lock, power and wiring first.

Can I see who came in?

Yes. The activity log shows every key accepted and every door unlocked.
