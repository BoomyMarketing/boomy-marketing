Is it right for your home?

A wired reader, not a gadget.

SmartphoneKey is a reader installed beside the door that switches an electric lock. That suits some homes better than others.

A good fit if

  • Several people come and go and each should have their own key.
  • You want timed keys for guests, sitters, cleaners and trades.
  • Someone in the household would rather use a card than a phone.
  • You want an activity log and a proper installation.

Maybe not if

  • You want a battery smart lock that you fit yourself.
  • Power and wiring can’t be brought to the door.
  • You rent and can’t change the door or its hardware.

Which door?

A house door, or a unit in a building?

Who decides, and what the job involves, depends on where the door is.

House or townhouse

It’s your decision. A front door that only takes a regular key usually needs an electric strike or lock, low-voltage power and a cable route before the reader can work.

Condo or apartment unit

You can only consider your own unit door, and building rules and fire requirements still apply. The lobby and garage doors belong to the building; that is a job for your manager or board.

Smart home

Add it to Google Home or Apple Home.

SmartphoneKey’s installation guide describes adding the reader to both through Matter. It’s optional, and worth testing on your own setup.

Google Home

Add a new device in the Google Home app and scan the reader’s Matter QR code. The guide asks for the phone and the reader to be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Apple Home

Add an accessory in the Home app with the Matter code. If the reader connects over Thread, the guide says you need a Thread border router such as a HomePod mini or Apple TV 4K.

Answers for homes

What homeowners ask first.

Plain answers, including where a store-bought lock might suit you better.

How is this different from a smart lock I buy at a store?

A store smart lock usually replaces the deadbolt and runs on batteries. SmartphoneKey is a wired reader beside the door that switches an electric lock, with Wallet keys, guest keys, cards and an activity log in one admin. Which suits you depends on your door and what you need.

Do I need an app?

SmartphoneKey describes Wallet keys as working from Apple Wallet or Google Wallet without a separate app. You manage keys in SmartphoneKey’s admin.

I lost my phone.

Remove that phone’s key in the admin and issue a new one. The activity log shows whether the key was used.

Can the kids use it without a phone?

SmartphoneKey key cards work on the same reader, and each card can be revoked on its own.

What happens in a power cut?

It depends on the lock type chosen for your door. The installer explains how your door behaves without power and plans a backup way in.

I live in a condo.

You can only consider your own unit door, and the building’s rules still apply. The lobby and garage are the building’s decision; the condos and buildings page is written for managers and boards.

What does it cost?

It depends on your door and any electrical work, so there is no price on this site. Cost is explained after the door check, including any ongoing costs.
