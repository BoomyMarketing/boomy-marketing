In short

How does keyless check-in work without a lockbox?

For airbnb hosts and short-term rental managers

For each stay you create a guest key that starts at check-in and ends at check-out, and send it to the guest by email or message. Cleaners get their own key for the turnover window. You issue the keys yourself, because an automatic link with Airbnb or other booking platforms isn’t confirmed.

What you get

The features that matter for short-term rentals.

  • Keys

    Guest keys with start and end times

    Give a visitor a key that starts and ends when you set. Limit how many times it opens, or tie it to one email.

  • Keys

    One-use keys

    A key for a single visit, such as a repair or a delivery. It opens once, then it is spent.

  • Keys

    One key, several doors

    A single key can cover more than one lock, for example a lobby and a unit door, or a front door and a back door.

  • Keys

    Keys reach offline locks

    If a lock is offline when you issue a key, the key waits as pending and is delivered when the lock is back online.

  • People & sites

    Remove access in seconds

    Remove a key, or deactivate a person entirely: their lock access ends and their portal sign-in is disabled.

  • Records & control

    Activity log

    Every key accepted, door unlocked, and key added or removed, filterable by lock, person, event and date.

  • Doors & devices

    Wi-Fi or built-in LTE

    The Wi-Fi model joins your network. The LTE model uses a SIM card where Wi-Fi does not reach, or on temporary sites.

  • Visitors

    QR intercom at the door

    Visitors scan the QR code on the reader with their phone camera. It opens in the browser and connects them to the right person to ask for entry.

  • Doors & devices

    Live lock status

    Each lock shows whether it is online or offline, so problems are visible before someone is stuck outside.

The door check

What we check at your door.

SmartphoneKey switches electric locks. Whether your door already has one, or needs one, decides what the job involves.

  1. 1

    The lock you have

    Regular key only, electric strike, magnetic lock or an old fob system.

  2. 2

    Power at the door

    The reader and the lock need low-voltage power nearby.

  3. 3

    The cable route

    How wiring reaches the lock and the power supply.

  4. 4

    How people leave

    An exit button is included; exit and safety needs are confirmed on site.

  5. 5

    Wi-Fi or LTE

    Dependable Wi-Fi at the door, or the LTE model with a SIM card.

  6. 6

    A backup way in

    What guests and you do if a phone dies, a link is lost or the network drops.

Condo unit? Your guests may still need to get through the lobby, and that door is the building’s decision. Building rules and short-term rental rules apply separately from the lock.

Is it the right fit?

Good for many. Not for every door.

A good fit if

  • You manage several doors or units and want one admin for all of them.
  • You want guest keys, Wallet keys and cards side by side.
  • You want an activity log and QR visitor calls at the door.

Maybe not if

  • You need keys created automatically from your bookings.
  • You only want a battery smart lock on one door that you fit yourself.
  • You can’t add power or change the door hardware.

Answers

What short-term rentals ask first.

Straight answers, including the ones that aren’t a simple yes.

Does it sync with Airbnb?

An automatic link with Airbnb or other booking platforms isn’t confirmed. You create and send each guest key yourself.

Do guests need an app?

No. A guest key works from a link, or from Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

A guest checks in late or leaves early.

Change the key’s times, or remove it, from the admin portal.

What if the Wi-Fi goes down?

The LTE model uses a SIM card. If a lock is offline when you issue a key, the key is delivered when the lock comes back online.

Can I see when the guest arrived?

Yes. The activity log shows when keys were accepted and the door unlocked.
