In short

What is phone door access for a gym?

For Gym owners, studio managers and front-desk leads

It lets members open the gym door by tapping their phone, with the key stored in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. The owner adds and removes members in an admin portal, sends trial visitors a key that ends on its own, and sees every entry in an activity log. Staff no longer have to guard the door at opening.

What you get

The features that matter for gyms & studios.

  • Keys

    Apple Wallet & Google Wallet keys

    The key lives in the phone’s Wallet. People tap it on the reader, with no separate app for everyday entry.

  • Keys

    Guest keys with start and end times

    Give a visitor a key that starts and ends when you set. Limit how many times it opens, or tie it to one email.

  • Keys

    One-use keys

    A key for a single visit, such as a repair or a delivery. It opens once, then it is spent.

  • Keys

    NFC key cards

    Physical cards for people who would rather not use a phone. Cards are programmed and revoked from the admin portal.

  • Keys

    Weekly lock schedules

    Set the hours each lock accepts keys, day by day, and sync the schedule to the door.

  • People & sites

    Remove access in seconds

    Remove a key, or deactivate a person entirely: their lock access ends and their portal sign-in is disabled.

  • People & sites

    Local admins

    Let a manager, front desk or superintendent run one site. You can hand them a set number of Wallet Keys to give out.

  • Records & control

    Activity log

    Every key accepted, door unlocked, and key added or removed, filterable by lock, person, event and date.

  • Doors & devices

The door check

What we check at your door.

A reader decides who can open the door. It does not stop someone following a member in, and it does not replace a plan for unstaffed hours. Talk these through at the door check.

  1. 1

    The lock you have

    Regular key only, electric strike, magnetic lock or an old fob system.

  2. 2

    Power at the door

    The reader and the lock need low-voltage power nearby.

  3. 3

    The cable route

    How wiring reaches the lock and the power supply.

  4. 4

    How people leave

    An exit button is included; exit and safety needs are confirmed on site.

  5. 5

    Wi-Fi or LTE

    Dependable Wi-Fi at the door, or the LTE model with a SIM card.

  6. 6

    Who needs keys

    Members, coaches, cleaners and trial visitors, and who would rather use a card.

Is it the right fit?

Good for many. Not for every door.

A good fit if

  • You add and remove members every week.
  • You want staff off door duty at opening.
  • You want a record of who came in and when.

Maybe not if

  • You need access created automatically from membership or billing software. That link isn’t confirmed.
  • Your entrance can’t take an electric lock or low-voltage power.

Answers for gyms

What gyms & studios ask first.

Straight answers, including the ones that aren’t a simple yes.

Does it connect to our membership or billing software?

A ready-made link isn’t confirmed. Plan on managing access in SmartphoneKey’s own admin portal, and bring it up at the door check.

Will access switch off when a membership ends?

When you remove the key or deactivate the person, their access ends. It doesn’t happen automatically from your billing system.

What about members who don’t want to use a phone?

Program an NFC key card for them. Cards work on the same reader and are revoked from the admin portal.

Can staff manage keys without the owner?

Yes. Make a manager a local admin for your location, and give them a set number of Wallet Keys to hand out.

We have more than one location.

Each location can be its own site in the admin portal, and you switch between them from one sign-in.
