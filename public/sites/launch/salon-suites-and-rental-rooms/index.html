In short

How do you give each renter access to only their own room?

For Owners of salon suites, rental rooms and hourly studios

Each renter gets one key, in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, that opens the front door and only their own suite. Hourly bookings get a guest key that works for the booked hours and then stops. When a lease ends, the owner removes the key instead of changing the lock, and the log shows every entry.

  • Renters change every few months, or book by the hour.
  • You want each renter to open only their own room.
  • You want to know who came in, and when.

What you get

The features that matter for salon suites & rental rooms.

  • One key, several doors

    A single key can cover more than one lock, for example a lobby and a unit door, or a front door and a back door.

  • Apple Wallet & Google Wallet keys

    The key lives in the phone’s Wallet. People tap it on the reader, with no separate app for everyday entry.

  • Guest keys with start and end times

    Give a visitor a key that starts and ends when you set. Limit how many times it opens, or tie it to one email.

  • One-use keys

    A key for a single visit, such as a repair or a delivery. It opens once, then it is spent.

  • QR intercom at the door

    Visitors scan the QR code on the reader with their phone camera. It opens in the browser and connects them to the right person to ask for entry.

  • Remove access in seconds

    Remove a key, or deactivate a person entirely: their lock access ends and their portal sign-in is disabled.

  • Weekly lock schedules

    Set the hours each lock accepts keys, day by day, and sync the schedule to the door.

  • Activity log

    Every key accepted, door unlocked, and key added or removed, filterable by lock, person, event and date.

  • Audit log

    A separate record of what admins did: who issued or removed which key, for whom, and when.

Answers

What salon suites & rental rooms ask first.

Can one key open the front door and one suite?

Yes. A single key can cover several locks, so a renter gets the front door and their own suite on one key.

How do hourly bookings work?

Create a guest key with the booking’s start and end time, and optionally a limit on how many times it opens. You issue it yourself.

Do renters need an app?

No separate app for everyday entry. Keys live in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

What if a renter leaves early?

Remove their key. The audit log keeps a record of who removed it and when.
